Project overview

A reusable GIS workflow for linear infrastructure planning.

This sample project demonstrates common GIS workflows and analyses used in project planning. It models a fictional Interstate 269 extension through Tennessee and Arkansas; the scripts can also be adapted to other infrastructure projects, including:

  • Roads
  • Railroads
  • Powerlines
  • Pipelines
  • Telecommunications lines
  • Fiber optic lines

Two corridor options are under consideration:

North Option

Runs northwest from Millington, bypasses Meeman-Shelby Forest State Park to the north, and connects to Interstate 55 north of Turrell, Arkansas.

South Option

Runs southwest from Millington, bypasses Meeman-Shelby Forest State Park to the south, and continues toward West Memphis, Arkansas, ending at the I-55 and I-40 interchange.
